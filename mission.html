How to use ↓
Start here · 1 minute
Plan a future science walk across Jezero Crater and return to base.
  1. Choose science stops below. The green square is your base; the orange circles are stops. Drag them to explore another plan.
  2. Press Calculate EVA to review the route, time, distance, return reserve, and alerts.
  3. Adjust suit settings or choose Obstacle, mark a spot on the map, and calculate again. Compare the faster and gentler routes.
  4. Open Data quality to see the source resolution and planning assumptions.
